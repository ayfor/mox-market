# Attribution footer and recommendation disclaimer (ruling R6, ship-blocker)

Source: vault note `software--mox-market-attribution-affiliate-pack` Part A (wording re-verified against the live Fan Content Policy page 2026-08-17) and `software--mox-market-legal-landscape` §5.2. This file is the implementer's copy. Change it only by changing the vault note first.

## Site footer, every page

Three stacked lines of plain, always-visible text. No hover, no expandable, no modal.

> Mox Market is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved/endorsed by Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the Coast. ©Wizards of the Coast LLC.

"Fan Content Policy" links to https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy

> This product uses TCGplayer data but is not endorsed or certified by TCGplayer.

> Card prices are daily estimates from third-party sources and are not guaranteed. Recommendations are automated informational signals, not financial, investment, or purchasing advice.

Note: the vault wording says "Verdicts"; the product vocabulary is "Recommendations" (Standards convention). The substitution is the only edit.

## Recommendation-adjacent disclaimer

Placed next to the recommendation UI itself (the panel on `/evaluate`), not only in the footer:

> Recommendations are automated informational signals derived from third-party price data that updates daily. They are estimates, not personalized financial, investment, or purchasing advice, and accuracy is not guaranteed. You are responsible for your own purchase decisions.

## At affiliate launch (not Phase 1)

Add a fourth footer line and per-link "Paid link" markers adjacent to retailer buttons:

> When you buy through links on this site, Mox Market may earn a commission. Affiliate programs include: [name only the programs actually enrolled].

Placement rules (FTC 16 CFR 255.5 and Competition Bureau, binding): disclosure visible on the same screen as the link without any click to expand; adjacent beats footer; a linked disclosures page supplements, never replaces, the adjacent marker; never write copy that contradicts the disclosure.

## Image rules (Scryfall and WotC)

Card images render unfiltered: no distortion, desaturation, colour shift, overlay, or cropping of the copyright and artist line. Art crops require artist name and © in the same interface. Never use the Scryfall name or logo in a way that implies endorsement.
